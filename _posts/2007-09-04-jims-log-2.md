Taken with 9.250 Celestron with F6.3 focal reducer and ST6b by SBIG. 30second exposure. A Meade LXD75 mount using TheSkyPro and CCDSoft5 for the imager. These were taken last night at over 12midnight to 3am. Even though I’m recovering from stroke I stayed out when I’m not supposed to. I’m getting better at using the software which is pretty intense. M57 is an exploding star and so is the Dumbell Nebula..NGC is New General Catalog. Some star clusters are open and some are closed.
